About Me

Green Economy and Just Transitions researcher with 9+ years designing research, strategy, and financing architectures for inclusive climate transitions in India, with deep focus on MSMEs, gender-responsive climate action, and financing pathways for an equitable low-carbon future.

I use a systems lens to connect climate policy, finance, equity, and livelihoods across India's key transition sectors and geographies.

Areas of Focus

Just Transition & Green Finance

Financing architectures and philanthropic strategy for coal-to-clean and low-carbon transitions in India, MSME access to capital and climate finance flows, sector-specific just transition pathways across AFOLU, textiles, and built environment.

MSMEs & Climate Resilience

Research and strategy on climate-resilient and inclusive micro-enterprises, decarbonisation pathways for MSME clusters, green economy country strategies, financing gap assessments, livelihoods in transition.

Gender-responsive Climate Action

Gendered pathways in climate and just transitions, women's economic empowerment in low-carbon sectors, equity-centred frameworks for climate programmes, gender-responsive enterprise development.

Climate risk and resilience financing, innovative finance instruments for green transitions, ESG disclosures (GRI, BRSR, IR), corporate sustainability strategy, sustainable supply chains.

Mixed-methods policy research, landscape analyses, theory of change design, evidence synthesis, policy briefs, reports and articles for practitioner and policy audiences.
